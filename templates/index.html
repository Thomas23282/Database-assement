The Starling is a New Zealand 9-foot-6-inch (2.90 m) sailing dinghy designed by Des Townson. It is a popular youth class dinghy in New Zealand. This website contains the results for the 2026 Starling Nationals regatta held at Worser Bay boating club, Wellington, New Zealand.
